What Is Notify and How Does It Work?
Source: Resy Help Desk (https://helpdesk.resy.com/what-is-notify-and-how-does-it-work-BJrJzPQLu)

Notify is Resy's modern waitlist, designed to turn cancellations into new booking opportunities. When a restaurant is fully booked for the day and time you want, you don't have to keep refreshing the reservations page. Instead, you add yourself to Notify and let Resy watch for openings on your behalf.

How it works: You select the restaurant, date, party size, and the time range you'd like, then turn on Notify for that slot. If another guest cancels or the restaurant releases additional inventory, Resy sends you an alert through the app or by email letting you know a table has opened up. When that notification goes out, you can claim the reservation and book it right away.

Important details to know:
- Notify is not a guarantee of a table. Alerts go out when availability opens, and reservations can be claimed quickly, so you need to act fast when you get the notification.
- You can set Notify on more than one date or time range to improve your odds for a hard-to-book restaurant.
- Keep notifications enabled on your phone so you don't miss the alert. For very popular restaurants, openings can be claimed within minutes.
- Some restaurants do not participate in Notify. For those, your best route to a last-minute seat is the in-person waitlist or walking in.

Notify is most useful for restaurants where reservations sell out the moment they drop. Setting Notify lets you catch the steady trickle of cancellations that happen as a reservation date approaches, which is often easier than competing for the initial drop.
